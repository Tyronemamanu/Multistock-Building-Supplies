About

Providing excellent customer service, with over 20 years industry experience.

At Multistock Building Resources, we’re here to help bring your building and renovation ideas to life with a can-do attitude.

From our Somerville showroom, we supply flooring, carpet, tiles, bathroom ware, tapware and kitchenware at wholesale prices, direct to the public. We’re here for builders, renovators and homeowners.

We offer quality products at great prices, along with friendly, personal service. Tell us your build and we’ll help you choose products to suit your space and budget, from your first enquiry through to delivery.
